SEA TURTLE FACTS

Sea Turtle Facts is a two-screen app about the pawikan (sea turtle).

HOME SCREEN (MainActivity, activity_main.xml)
When the app starts, the home screen opens. From top to bottom it shows:
  - the title "Sea Turtle Facts"
  - the subtitle "Meet the Pawikan"
  - a picture of a sea turtle
  - a "Learn More" button below the picture

FACTS SCREEN (FactsActivity, activity_facts.xml)
Tapping "Learn More" opens the second screen. It shows the title "Sea Turtles",
then the Habitat facts, then the Threats facts. Each heading is followed by its own list.
